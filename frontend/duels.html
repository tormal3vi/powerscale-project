How it works

  1. Challenge anyone, or one user by name. Pick up to five matchups yourself, or let them be drawn at random from close tiers.
  2. Each round shows two characters, with no stats. You have 20 seconds to call who the site says wins: its verdict, or an admin's overrule.
  3. You each play on your own time. Answers stay hidden until both of you are done.
  4. More right answers wins. The same count is a draw. Unplayed rounds count as wrong after 3 days.
