How it works

  1. Pick a format (1v1, free-for-all or teams) and open it to anyone, or invite players by name. Choose up to five matchups yourself, or let them be drawn at random from close tiers.
  2. Each round shows two characters, with no stats. You have 20 seconds to call who the site says wins: its verdict, or an admin's overrule.
  3. Everyone plays on their own time, from the moment they join. Answers stay hidden until everyone is done.
  4. Most right answers wins. In teams, members' answers add up. A shared top score is a draw. Unplayed rounds count as wrong 3 days after the others finish.
