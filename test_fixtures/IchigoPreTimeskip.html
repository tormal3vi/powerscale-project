Summary[]

Ichigo Kurosaki (黒崎くろさき 一いち護ご, Kurosaki Ichigo?) is the son of Masaki and Isshin Kurosaki, a Quincy with the powers of a Soul Reaper. Years after the death of his mother, Ichigo uses his innate spiritual awareness to help local ghosts, and unlocks his potential as a Soul Reaper when Rukia Kuchiki comes along in pursuit of a Hollow.

Gifted

Ichigo may be straightforward at times and can seem clueless, but underneath a base level interpretation, Ichigo is exceptionally intelligent. Before Bleach, he was an excellent student grade wise, ranking near the top 20 in his school[106]. He was able to remain above the average in high school despite the pre-timeskip events of Bleach interfering with his life, only having dropping grades due to his depressed state after his loss of powers, which all of those events occurred while he was in school both physically and emotionally. Even before the events of the story, he was known for constantly skipping school, which persisted into high school as well[107][108]. but despite that, he scored high among his class. He deduced his Shihakusho is a part of his Bankai on his own and how it indicates his current Reiatsu and further confirmed this after Orihime told him Reiatsu takes longer to recover, noting to Unohana that his current Reiatsu was indicated by his Shihakusho's current condition[78]. He also mastered his true Hollow and Quincy powers and understands the equilibrium and what is needed to breach it after just unlocking his newfound abilities[21]. Ichigo also within only days of being a Soul Reaper, could use Spirit Ribbons without any guidance at all, a feat not even Rukia could do despite her years of experience within the Gotei 13[109]. His knowledge is vast compared to many as he was taught by Kisuke Urahara, the greatest genius the Soul Society ever had, learning about the cosmology, races, techniques, and even learning about some of the Soul Society's top secrets, something most captains don't know, in addition to other sources such as the Vizards, Xcution, and Squad 0 teaching him things such as Hollowfication, Fullbrings, and the Royal Palace, being able to quickly absorb and apply that information despite these being completely foreign to him as a teenager with no experience with phenomenon beyond the World of the Living and having no official or formal teachings of it, in addition to his vast lack of quantitative experience compared to most Soul Reapers. He very quickly learns new life skills and general concepts and is able to apply them with ease such as learning how to fly using Yoruichi's device after being told how to fly once without training[110].

Ichigo is infamously adaptive and has a staggering growth rate, being listed as a war potential for his extraordinary growth speed in combat and training[11]. He can go from being completely inferior in skill and stats to outskilling his enemies and overpowering them quickly. He learned Bankai in 2 days using a life-threatening method when even prodigies need 10 years to do so, gaining 10 years of experience in only 2 days[111][112][12][113][43][114][115]. After the first day, it was stated by Yoruichi that Ichigo's combat skill exclusively had improved, and was also stated by Urahara that Ichigo is more gifted than he is[12], which holds significant weight as Urahara is a combat genius in his own right. Ichigo has also faced off against many unique and strange weapons and have used them to his advantage through adapting and analyzing as well as honing his skills and instincts[116][49][50][1][117]. Despite the massive barrier between Byakuya and Renji, Ichigo surpassed this barrier easily within his first few fights by fighting equally with Byakuya after just recently fighting Renji only two fights ago, even outskilling him[13]. While vastly weaker than him, he learned to control his Reiatsu flow against Kenpachi and went from not even making a dent and lacking the ability to land a meaningful hit, to dealing actual damage to him[23]. He became a master of Flash Step in a matter of minutes, a technique that takes 6 years to master[118][13][54], being able to spam it relentlessly in his fight with Byakuya, in addition to effectively using his Bankai just after he unlocked it. In his first fight after his only 10 day training, he quickly adapted to Ikkaku's unique weapon and beat him utilizing his skills.[49] Ichigo learned everything about his 1st Fullbring near the end of his first fight after unlocking it such as utilizing a long range attack similar to Getsuga Tenshō[119] and learned about it's weaknesses in the second real battle and quickly used said weaknesses to his advantage[22]. During his battle with Ulquiorra, he was struggling to do anything against a base Ulquiorra without his weapon drawn, being unable to read anything about the Hollow or predict his moves, but then he was able to read him, noting that it didn't matter whether or not he drew his sword and was able to read his attacks by sensing his emotions as he noted Ulquiorra started to become more human[2]. In the final battle against Yhwach, Ichigo unlocked Horn of Salvation in combat with a plan to use Yhwach's Reiatsu to activate it with Orihime giving him support, showing that his combat intelligence had improved from just running into battle[21].

Ichigo is an extraordinary prodigy at swordsmanship, being able to duel enemies with vastly more quantitative and qualitative experience, ranging from multiple centuries to milienia, and hold his own even with a stat disadvantage. His weapon of choice is a giant unwieldly sword that he uses with great agility and skill, being able to also take advantage of its large size. In Bankai, he uses a traditional katana, which is massively different than his Shikai when it comes to functionality, and learned to do so on the spot against a master swordsman with over 150 years of experience[56]. He became a proficient master of Zanjutsu after only 10 days of learning it through combat with Kisuke[49]. After said training he kept up in combat with Ikkaku Madarame, the third seat of squad 11, known for their combat skill while fighting a weapon that was completely alien to him, and using said weapon to vault off and strike him while upside down mid-backflip[116]. After only 2 fights post-training, he fought equally[120][23] and stalemated[121] Kenpachi Zaraki, a swordsman who gave Unohana the hardest battle she had[122], while being a mere child who had only recently gained an Asauchi and awakened a Zanpakuto. Unohana being a swordswoman with over two millennia of experience and having mastered 8000 sword styles[123] and bestowed the name "Kenpachi", a title fit for the strongest Soul Reaper, and who had originally defeated Zaraki while being the physically weaker of the two[122]. He was able to deflect a hundred million[124] of Senbonzakura's petals coming at him from all sides with a single blade[15], each one as thin as paper[125]. In his brief fight with Nnoitra, the Espada's best swordsman superior to other masters such as Ulquiorra and Starrk, he was able to change his tactics after he stated he had observed Ichigo through his fight with Grimmjow, keeping himself alive even while fatigued from his previous fight, even dodging his attacks while distracted[103][126][127]. Ichigo after nearly 2 years of no fighting mastered his new Bankai, which was the shape and size of a Nodachi, being significantly longer than his old Bankai. While in said Bankai against Ginjo, he took advantage of his large weapon by utilizing his speed thanks to his smaller one and broke his sword by turning it sideways to increase surface area and killed him[16][17]. Quilge Opie, the leader of the Jagdarmee and the combat director of the Sternritters[128], had openly admitted that he was less skilled than Ichigo after he grabbed his arrow with his bare hand and deflected multiple arrows while offguard[129]. After realizing his true self and having reached an understanding of his existence, he mastered dual wielding two large, impractical swords with no training or experience, using said swords to fight Yhwach toe to toe, a foe made up of the memories and skills of potentially 10 trillion souls that was a threat to even Yamamoto in his prime[130], a Soul Reaper who for 1000 years had no rival among natural born Soul Reapers, having mastered every combat art[13], massively superior even to Unohana, as well as clash with Ichibē Hyōsube, a spearman with over 1 million years of experience and knowledge as well as the very man who created Bankai and named every concept within the Soul Society,[131] and a master of sacred Kido.

Although he mostly uses his swords, Ichigo is a very capable hand to hand combatant. He was taught Karate by Tatsuki's family[132] and later went on to surpass[108] Tatsuki as a martial artist, who won 2nd place in a nationals tournament despite having a broken arm having lost to an opponent who is far stronger physically, stating she would've won had it not been for it[133]. He also fights with his dad on a near daily basis who is trained in Hakuda as a basic Soul Reapers. He has been constantly shown to beat multiple armed thugs with just hand to hand[120] and even take out 3 lieutenants with it[20]. He is capable of grappling his foes to the ground and attack them while they're pinned.[134]

Ichigo can analyze an opponent's movements and predict when he can make a perfectly timed strike through reading their emotions[2], killing intent, and through seeing how they look onto him[9][135], even when he's losing blood in his head and starts to become dizzy.[1] He was able to analyze how many slashes Renji had a limit of using and was able to predict where he would land them for a surefire counterattack and can gauge and predict opponent's attack, defense, speed, and reactions, allowing him to properly read Ulquiorra.[1][2] Ichigo had mimicked some of White's tactics such as blocking and using Getsuga Tenshō to back his opponent off, and also using Getsuga Tenshō to close in and use another while behind them after only seeing them once.[57][102][30][136][25][69][65] He was able to fully pull apart Gin's plan to deceive Ichigo through using wanton destruction, unnecessary info, and was able to deduce that his Bankai's retraction speed is faster than his piercing speed in only 2 interactions, also noting that he was still in Bankai despite the sword looking the same as it's Shikai, despite being fed both false and irrelevant information[9][10]. Against Quilge, he noticed that Quilge had eased up on his blade when he blocked his attack while bragging, showing that he recognized Quilge's overconfidence and had taken advantage by backing him off[129]. Later in that same fight, he quickly recognized that his halo was the source of his Reishi subjugation and he broke it in response to him using it to become more powerful.[8] He was constantly analyzing Yhwach's Almighty, looking for a weakness while he was being underestimated.[18]

While not armed with a vast kit, only notably having one move, Ichigo has used Getsuga Tenshō ingeniously to suit multiple scenarios. He used Getsuga Tenshō defensively against Dordoni by backing him off with limited mobility akin to a baseball bunt[60] and against Yhwach when he used it as an AoE to dispel Qualkreis[137]. Against Candice, he controlled Getsuga Tenshō to make it stationary to block her attack in addition to being able to control its area of effect, then later combined two to create Getsuga Jujishō[138]. Offensively, he has used it to boost the power of his strikes against Ulquiorra by wrapping his sword in it without discharging it to make up for his lacking firepower[26], and against Yhwach, he combined a Gran Rey Cero with a Getsuga Tenshō, having only seen it once 2 years prior to the fight and after a 17 month hiatus from his duties, impressing the war genius.[21]

Ichigo has an extremely high spatial awareness in battle, using sound and intuition to remain aware in battle. He was able to notice Kenpachi's Bells and used them to stop him from getting perception blitzed[117]. While blinded, he kept up with him by using his presence and sound to locate him[139]. He has been shown to dodge attacks with his instinct alone such as almost every day when his dad surprise attacks him, against Ulquiorra, and after he got healed by Tenjiro, with the latter event being able to parry and counter him. These reflexes are impressive enough to be able to dodge attacks while meditating or in a deep sleep[3][4][5][6][7]. Ichigo is also constantly aware of blitzing techniques such as Flash Step and Hirenkyaku, being able to react to them appropriately[56].


Weaknesses: Ichigo is brash and can rush into fights without thinking, and he can also be too merciful or have his powers limited by his lack of fighting spirit. He can only use his Hollow Mask for so long, originally for four to eleven seconds though this eventually becomes moot. Using Mugetsu will strip him of all his Soul Reaper powers and cause him to fall unconscious for a month

Notable Attacks/Techniques:

Flash Step (瞬しゅん歩ぽ, Shunpo?): An application of Hohō used by Soul Reapers, allowing for high-speed movement, much faster than the eye can follow, used in combat and travel, typically in short bursts. The skill can be summarized as taking the least amount of steps to reach point B from point A. While he makes skillful use of Flash Step, Ichigo is not particularly well-versed in the specifics of the technique and his use of it is fairly straightforward.

Zangetsu (斬月ざんげつ?): Ichigo's Zanpakutō, which in its original, unreleased state, took the form of a very oversized katana, thanks to his immense but poorly-controlled spiritual power. Because of this, the sword itself was rather weak at this point, and it is more a manifestation of Rukia's deprived spiritual energy than Ichigo's. Once he learns its name, Ichigo's Zanpakutō stays in a constant released state due to his immense power.

Pseudo Zanpakutō: Shikai (始し解かい?): After first being released, Zangetsu appeared as a massive, knife-like blade with no formal hilt, only a cloth-wrapped tang. In this form, Zangetsu is about as tall as Ichigo, and he can wield it by the cloth to swing and throw it opponents like a flail, though he rarely does this. When he reclaims his Soul Reaper powers and combines them with his Fullbring abilities, Zangetsu's appearance shifts, giving it a more proper hilt and a length of chain at the bottom.

  • Getsuga Tenshō (月げつ牙が天てん衝しょう?): Ichigo's signature attack, a highly condensed blast of spiritual pressure released from the tip of the blade with a slash, taking the form of a crescent as it rushes forwards, or even an omnidirectional burst. Though Ichigo uses this ability several times before finding out its name, once he does, its power is greatly enhanced.
Getsuga Tenshō

Bankai (卍解ばんかい?): The ultimate and final release to a Zanpakutō. The difference in power between Shikai and Bankai is generally around 5-10 times more powerful. Even though Bankai is the final stage of a Zanpakutō, it does not mean the Soul Reaper's growth ends there. A Bankai, especially one that has been recently obtained, can evolve even further as its Soul Reaper also strengthens.

  • Tensa Zangetsu (天てん鎖さ斬月ざんげつ?): Ichigo's Bankai is activated by pointing his Zanpakutō outwards and allowing its cloth to wrap around his arm, Ichigo's Tensa Zangetsu is very out of the ordinary for a Bankai. It shrinks Ichigo's normally massive sword down to a daitō, a long sword, with a black blade, focusing and compressing all of its power for use in hyper-speed combat. The appearance of his Bankai shifts after his training in the Dangai, covering his right hand with a black glove and wrapping its chain all around his arm.
    • Black Getsuga (黒くろい月げつ牙が, Kuroi Getsuga?): The more powerful Getsuga Tenshō utilized by Ichigo in this state, now colored black. Ichigo can utilize it with more control than before, controlling its trajectory, keeping it within his blade to enhance the power of his swings, or firing it from the tip as a bullet.
Black Getsuga Tenshō

Final Getsuga Tenshō (最さい後ごの月げつ牙が天てん衝しょう, Saigo no Getsuga Tenshō?): The ultimate technique of Ichigo's Pseudo Bankai, in which he becomes one with Getsuga (月げつ牙が?) itself. Its name, the Final Getsuga Tenshō reflects its cost - after using it, Ichigo will lose all of his Soul Reaper powers. While the ability is active, Ichigo's hair turns black and grows to waist-length, while his body is wrapped in blue-grey bandages.

Final Getsuga Tenshō
  • Mugetsu (無む月げつ?): Ichigo manifests a black blade from his spiritual energy and swings it at his target, bringing with it a massive veil of black energy that erupts upwards, completely consuming them and the landscape. After this, Ichigo will lose the transformation and quickly fall unconscious, awakening a month later with only a fragment of his powers, which will quickly dwindle to nothing, but he slowly regained them over the next two years.
Mugetsu

Hollowfication (虚ホロウ化か, Horō-ka?): Thanks to White, Ichigo was born with the powers of a Hollow, which he can channel in the form of a skull-like Hollow mask to greatly increase his capabilities. Originally, he could not control this transformation and would subconsciously produce the mask to shield himself from attacks. As well he would be possessed by his inner Hollow if he fell unconscious, but after his training with the Visored, he gained more control over the transformation. Still, in particularly dire circumstances, his inner Hollow may take control, and Ichigo may enter a form where he loses all sense of reason.

Hollow Mask: The Hollow mask was originally a sign of Ichigo's possession by his inner Hollow, but after his training with the Visored, it merely indicates he is utilizing his Hollow powers. To access his inner Hollow's power, Ichigo must summon the mask onto his face. He normally does this by placing one of his hands over his face and concentrating spiritual matter on it to manifest the mask (previously, it had appeared on its own before he mastered the Hollow within him), summoning the mask in a "ripping" fashion. With the mask in place, Ichigo's eyes irises become yellow, and he has black sclera and pupils. His voice has a slight Hollow echo to it. With the mask, he gains access to increased physical enhancements. This helps in combat in several ways, most noticeably being that it makes it easier for him to fight through severe injuries.

1st Hollow Mask
2nd Hollow Mask
  • Augmented Getsuga Tenshō (月げつ牙が天てん衝しょう?): When Ichigo wears his mask, the power, speed, blast area, force, and size of the Getsuga Tenshō become drastically augmented, to the point of seriously harming a foe. When used with his Bankai and Hollow mask in combination, his Getsuga Tenshō is stated to be his ultimate attack.
Augmented Getsuga Tenshō

Full Hollow Form: When Zangetsu takes full possession of Ichigo's body, the body undergoes a full transformation into a Hollow creature. His mask resembles a Human skull, complete with a jaw joint and rounded forehead, and has dark red stripes on the left side. Unlike normal Hollows, this form used an actual weapon, using Ichigo's Zanpakutō. Ichigo later has his body taken over by Zangetsu once again. This time, the full transformation is very physically different in appearance and far more powerful than the previous one. In this form, Ichigo has long straight hair, reaching down to his waist, and a Hollow hole, smaller than his previous one, decorated with markings stretching from the hole across his chest and shoulders. In this new form, he has a different Hollow mask. It covers Ichigo's entire head, and its markings are different, for it has four spike-shaped marks, two of them stopping by the forehead and the other two stretching past the eyes and teeth, going past his chin and joining with the marks on his chest. The mask features two long forward-pointing horns, with black strips extending from their center towards the tips.

1st Full Hollow Form
2nd Full Hollow Form
  • Cero (虚閃セロ, Sero?): The signature attack of Hollows, and one utilized by the Arrancar and Visored as well, a powerful blast of concentrated spiritual energy fired like a laser. Ichigo's version of the cero is red and is extremely powerful.
1st Cero
2nd Cero
  • Hierro (鋼皮イエロ, Iero?): A defensive technique used by Arrancar that toughens their skin by reinforcing it with condensed Reiryoku, allowing an Arrancar to confront an armed Soul Reaper with their bare hands. All Arrancar use this ability in some form to gain their supernatural toughness, but even an exceptionally powerful Hierro can be bypassed by focusing one's Reiryoku to a finer, sharper point.
  • High-Speed Regeneration (超ちょう速再生そくさいせい, Chōsoku Saisei?): An ability possessed by most Hollows, which allows them to rapidly recover from wounds. Abandoned by most Hollow and Arrancar in favor of more power, Ichigo retains the ability in his full Hollow form, proving capable of quickly regenerating lost limbs and even a hole in his chest, which would make it superior to the regenerative abilities of most others, as they cannot heal from serious organ damage.
High-Speed Regeneration
  • Sonído (響転ソニード, Sonīdo?): A technique used by Arrancar that allows for high-speed movement, effectively analogous to the Flash Step used by Soul Reapers, and used in bursts to traverse distances quickly and fight at these high speeds.
