Summary[]

Wendy Marvell is a young female Sky Dragon Slayer. She was one of the orphans chosen to be a Dragon Slayer four hundred years prior to X777, serving as her foster mother's vessel to travel to X777 through the Eclipse Gate as a part of the plan to exterminate Acnologia. Formerly belonging to the Cait Shelter Guild, she officially joins the Fairy Tail Guild after the events surrounding Nirvana unfold. Following the disbandment of Fairy Tail, Wendy, along with Carla, joined Lamia Scale. After the war between Orochi's Fin and Lamia Scale ended, Wendy left Lamia Scale and helped Natsu restore Fairy Tail. After the defeat of both Zeref and Acnologia in the original series, she accepted the 100 Years Quest with her team to defeat the Five Dragon Gods on Guiltina.

Sky Dragon Slayer Magic: Wendy is the Sky Dragon Slayer, which means that she has the same type of Magic as Natsu Dragneel and Gajeel Redfox, but with the ability to manipulate air. Her Magic also allows her to heal people with the gravest of injuries, even those caused by a Dragon. Her proficiency in healing allows her to use even a kiss to recover other people, and she can counteract other Magic. Her Magic also has support spells, increasing attack power and speed for herself and others. Carla states that Wendy can eat air but can't heal well in polluted areas. Her Magic also allows her to sense air currents, which can be used to predict the weather.

  • Sky Dragon's Roar: An air version of Dragon's Roar. When using this attack, Wendy breathes a hurricane-like blast at her target. After having her Second Origin unlocked, it is powerful enough to destroy one of Nirvana's Lacrima crystals and match Sherria's Sky God's Bellow. This spell can also be used with the additional power of Wendy's healing Magic. It has both offensive and healing properties, allowing Wendy to cure Erigor's memory block, which released the Magic bestowed upon him and rendered him unable to battle.
  • Heal: A healing Magic that is powerful and believed to be long-lost. The user manipulates clean air to perform various feats, such as draining the poison from an infected victim and even reviving people in a near-death state. During her battle with Cobra, Wendy used this spell to heal Erza of her poisoning after being bitten and poisoned by Cubellios.
  • 'Troia:A spherical healing Magic that Wendy used to heal Natsu's motion sickness. The spell's effects are temporary, and its power wanes with repeated castings. On a side note, Wendy will be unable to perform the spell if she, too, experiences motion sickness.
  • Sky Dragon's Wing Attack: This Magic features Wendy expelling a whirlwind of air from her arms to attack her surroundings. This Magic is a mimic of Natsu's Fire Dragon's Wing Attack
  • Sky Dragon's Claw: Wendy jumps and lifts her feet and unleashes large winds when the leg is swung down at the target.
  • Sky Dragon's Crushing Fang: Wendy charges and swipes the target with her fingers leaving a wake of wind behind as she does so.
  • Sky Dragon's Wave Wind: Wendy swings her hand and generates a powerful tornado.
  • Sky Dragon's Burst: Wendy gathers wind at her feet to boost their speed.
  • Sky Dragon's Breath: Wendy gathers wind in her hands and heals someone from a distance.

Dragon Force: By consuming air with a high concentration of Ethernano, Wendy can enter Dragon Force. This ability is said to be the final and most powerful state a Dragon Slayer can attain. It has been said that their power becomes comparable to that of a real Dragon. Wendy gains pink eyes together with longer hair, now pink instead of blue, that curves and points upwards. The Dragon Slayer also has white scales, similar to Grandeeney's, that spring out of her back and smaller ones on her hands and feet. After the one-year time skip, while fighting the Avatar army, Wendy was shown to be able to enter Dragon Force at will.

Wendy's Dragon Force

Enchantment: A large portion of the spells in Wendy's possession are enchantments, which allow her to attach her Magic Power to someone or something (Including herself) to boost their natural parameters. Being capable of such a feat makes Wendy, by default, in addition to being a Dragon Slayer, an "Enchanter." However, not all her enchantments are associated with Sky Dragon Slayer Magic. It has been noted that Wendy's aptitude for enchantments is exceptionally great, with her being able to cast enchantments on a level comparable to a High Enchanter's. Irene stated that Wendy's magical aptitude was much higher than expected, that when she controlled Wendy's body, she cast enchantments with equal power to her own.

  • Vernier: A spell that speeds up the target's movement, including the caster themselves, for a short time. Incantation of this Magic: "Fast wind that run the heaven... VERNIER..."
    • Ile Vernier: By chanting "Ile Vernier," Wendy can double someone's speed.
  • Arms: After reciting an enchantment, the caster targets a region of the body to increase damage output, such as the arms, for a short period. Incantation of this Magic: "Power of the stout arms that tears heaven... ARMS.."
    • Ile Arms: By chanting "Ile Arms", Wendy can double someone's attack power.
  • Armor: A spell that enhances the target's defense power, including the caster themself, for a short period.
    • Ile Armor: By chanting "Ile Armor," Wendy can double someone's durability.
  • Body Anomaly Reversal Magic, Raise: As seen when she reversed Kamika's binding spell, Wendy can use this spell to undo the effects of harmful Magic on herself or others.
    • Anomaly Resistance Enchant: Re-Raise: A supportive spell that will protect Wendy from any Magic of anomaly effect targeted towards her.
  • Deus Corona: By chanting "Elemental resistances rise!", Wendy can temporarily increase her defensive capabilities quite significantly.
  • Deus Eques: By chanting "Physical ability rise!", Wendy can temporarily increase her overall physical capabilities.
  • Deus Zero: Wendy can cast this high-level enchantment, which allows her to negate Magic and external enchantments. She used it to reject Irene Belserion's Deus Zero. She later demonstrated in Irene's body that she could completely nullify Irene's personality from her body and return it to where it belonged.
  • Personality Enchantment: This enchantment allows Wendy to augment a personality. Wendy uses this enchantment to temporarily transfer herself into Irene's body, thus becoming its host.
  • Magic Enchantment: Wendy taught this enchantment to Erza to allow her to attach Dragon Slayer Magic or Natsu and Gray's Magic on her swords.
  • Dragon Slayer Seal: By chanting her intentions, Wendy can enchant others with Dragon Slayer attribute from her Magic, as well as be able to stack multiple Dragon Slayer Magics from other Dragon Slayers. This Enchantment also lasts beyond its initial usage, as Erza could use it again on her own, days after she first gained it.
  • Residual Thought Enchantment: This type of Enchantment allows the user to enchant someone's residual thought or memory onto them as a power. If the spell is successful, the user will take on the characteristics of the person whose thoughts or memories they enchanted, including their appearance and clothing.
    • Enchantment: Irene Belserion: Wendy enchants Irene's residual thoughts, memories, and magic power onto herself and has her appearance change to be similar to Irene. Wendy also gained the ability to interact and see Irene's personality while using this enchantment since Irene implanted her essence in Wendy beforehand via Personality Enchantment.
  • Separation Enchantment: This enchantment allows Wendy to separate Magic from a person or object. With the guidance of Irene's personality enchanted on her, Wendy can use this enchantment to temporarily remove Nebaru's Magic to incapacitate him.
  • Conceptual Enchantment: This enchantment allows Wendy to focus on forging different types of attributes with other attributes, techniques, or people. Such as gathering Suspiria in the area to create a Spiritual Art that can enchant a person who uses Magic or an ordinary weapon.
