Summary[]

Yami Sukehiro (夜見やみ 介大すけひろ?) is the Captain of the Black Bulls, a squad of the Clover Kingdom's Magic Knights. A foreigner from "the Land of the Rising Sun" who washed up on the kingdom's shores after his boat capsized, Yami is uncouth, hypocritical, and prone to delivering death threats at the slightest provocation. Nevertheless, he is a powerful and well-respected sorcerer who was able to recognize Asta's merits when none of the other Captains would.

Dark Magic (闇やみ魔ま法ほう, Yami Mahō?): Yami's magic allows him to manipulate darkness. He primarily uses this magic to increase his physical abilities and channels it into his sword, creating destructive arcs of dark energy that are powerful enough to counter Licht's Light Magic spells. It is a rare and unique magic that was recognized by the Sorcery Emperor himself.

  • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Lightless Slash (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・無む明みょう斬ぎり』 Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Mumyōgiri"?) Channeling his magic into his katana, Yami swings his blade, unleashing a dark arc of energy that absorbs other attacks and retains the same sharpness as an edged weapon.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Lightless Blade Storm (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・無む明みょう乱みだれ斬ぎり』, Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Mumyō Midaregiri"?): Yami coats their sword in Dark Magic and swings it in a disorderly fashion, creating multiple Dark Cloaked Avidya Slashes.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Black Blade (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・黒くろ刃ば』, Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Kuroba"?) By coating his katana with darkness, Yami can increase the range and power of his swings.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Black Blade Blunt Slash (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・黒くろ刃ば峰みね薙なぎ』, Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Kuroba Minenagi"?): Yami coats a sword in darkness and extends the blade by shaping the darkness. Then he swings the blade wide and pushes a target back with the blunt side of the sword.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Lightless Blunt Strike (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・無む明みょう峰みね打うち』, Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Mumyō Mineuchi"?): Yami coats his sword with Dark Magic, goes behind their target, and strikes the target's nape with the back of their sword. The blow is strong enough to knock the target unconscious, and since it is done with the blunt side of the sword, the target is not grievously injured.
    • Dark Magic: Black Cocoon (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『黒繭くろまゆ』, Yami Mahō "Kuromayu"?): Yami creates a spherical shell of darkness around themselves. The sphere can also be used as a means of transport.
    • Dark Magic: Black Hole (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『黒穴くろあな』, Yami Mahō "Kuroana"?): Yami creates a singularity of darkness that draws in incoming projectile attacks, nullifying them in the process, but is limited by the singularity's small size.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Dimension Slash (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・次じ元げん斬ぎり』, Yami Mahō "Yamimatoi: Jigengiri"?): A powerful slash that cuts through "dimensions" (space) itself, which Yami described as being able to cut through anything. Yami developed this attack as he was trapped and pushed to the brink watching his men fight a battle to the death.
    • Mana Zone: Dark Cloaked Dimension Slash: Equinox: Greater version of Dark Cloaked Dimension Slash, extending its range and reach.
    • Mana Zone: Black Moon (魔何マナゾーン『黒月くろづき』, Mana Zōn "Kurozuki"?): Yami takes control of the surrounding mana and then creates a Black Hole overhead, expanding the range of its absorption over a defined area. Yami is also able to discriminate what gets absorbed by the spell, such as erasing an opponent's spell while leaving an ally's spell unaffected.
    • Mana Zone Condense: Dark Cloaked Iai Slash (魔何マナゾーン凝縮ぎょうしゅく『闇やみ纏まとい・居い合あい斬ぎり』, Mana Zōn "Iaigiri"?): Yami sheathes his sword, condenses the area of Black Moon's effect, and waits for his opponent to attack. Once the opponent is in range, Yami coats his sword in darkness, quickly draws the blade, and dashes forward, slashing the opponent across the stomach.
    • Dark Magic: Death Thrust (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『死し突とつ』, Yami Mahō "Shitotsu"?): Yami condenses the effect of Black Moon even further than his standard Mana Zone: Condense, focusing it onto one spot. He then thrusts his sword forward, creating a focused blast that's powerful enough to completely blow away 80% Dante's torso.
    • Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Deep Black Blade (闇やみ魔ま法ほう『闇やみ纏まとい・深黒しんくろ刃ば』, Yami Maho: "Yamimatoi: Shin Kuroba"?): By coating his blade with darkness, Yami can increase the range and power of his swings greater than Dark Magic: Dark Cloaked Black Blade.
  • Shadow Magic x Dark Magic: Kids' Playground (影かげ魔ま法ほう × 闇やみ魔ま法ほう『餓鬼がきの遊あそ戯場びば』, Kage Mahō x Yami Mahō "Gaki no Asobiba"?): With Mana Zone at full power, Yami and Nacht can create a spell that encompasses a large area with dark constructs used to hide themselves and allies from the enemy. This spell also creates multiple waves of dark clones that resemble Yami and Nacht to attack the enemy. Tactically, it is used to sneak up on enemies while the clones act as a distraction.

Ki (氣き?): Having been raised in the Eastern lands, Yami can read the natural energy given off by human beings to predict their next attacks, granting him a pseudo-sixth sense for danger and enhancing his reaction speed.

Mana Zone (魔何マナゾーン, Mana Zōn?): Offensively, the mana could be funneled into spells to increase their size and number. It could also be used to launch attacks from all-around, within, or behind a target. Defensively, the mana could be used to augment Mana Skin and other Reinforcement Magic spells, allowing for the prediction of enemies' attacks and for moving in mid-air.

Reinforcement Magic (強きょう化か魔ま法ほう, Kyōka Mahō?): Yami uses this magic to increase his physical abilities, such as speed, defense and attack force.

  • Mana Skin (魔何マナスキン, Mana Sukin?): Basic technique for every mage that increases their durability by coating their body with their own Magic Power.

Binding Magic (拘束こうそく魔ま法ほう, Kōsoku Mahō?): Is a form of magic that allows the users to immobilize their opponents.

  • Dark Binding Magic: Dark Restraints (闇拘束やみこうそく魔ま法ほう『闇やみの緊縛きんばく』, Kōsoku Mahō "Yami no Kinbaku"?): Yami creates ropes made of darkness which wrap around the target, restricting their movements and magic.
