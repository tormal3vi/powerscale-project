Summary[]

Shapesmith is a Martian with the ability to transform into almost anyone or anything, during the Invincible journey to Mars, Shapesmith snuck onto the astronauts' spaceship and replaced Rus Livingston (the main astronaut) later, after failing to fully embody Rus, he watched a TV special showing Martian Man being a hero which inspired him to become a hero.

After losing his job at N.A.S.A[1] he decided to use his superpowers to fight crime after seeing Martian Man on television.[2] During his first night out as a superhero he took down the Lizard League on his own.[3] He has become a member of The Guardians of the Globe, mainly because Shapesmith captured Doc Seismic.[4] When Abraham Lincoln and Kate Cha announced that they got married Cecil arrived to warn them that a ship from Mars was coming to Earth and Shapesmith got visibly nervous.[5] He confessed that he was actually a Martian and that the whole situation was his fault and accompanied the Guardians on the mission to stop the Sequids.[6] After arriving on the ship he revealed himself as a Martian but his compatriots attacked him as the Sequids rebellion was his fault but The Immortal defended him.[7] During the fight against the Sequids he shielded Robot with his body and gave him time to finish his weapon.[8]

Robot eventually killed Shapesmith when he took over the world.[9]
