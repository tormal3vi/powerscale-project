Summary[]

Sasuke Uchiha is one of the last surviving members of Konohagakure's Uchiha clan. Since his childhood, he made it his goal to exact revenge against his older brother, Itachi, who had slaughtered their entire clan.

He was assigned to Team 7, alongside Naruto Uzumaki, Sakura Haruno, and Kakashi Hatake. They managed to slowly warm up to him. However, after an encounter with Orochimaru, who branded him with a Curse Seal, in addition to his envy towards Naruto's rapid growth, and later, Itachi's provocation, he decides to abandon the Hidden Leaf in search of more power.

Weaknesses: Sasuke is somewhat overconfident and is easy to manipulate emotionally. The Sharingan's ability to copy Ninjutsu, Genjutsu, and Taijutsu is limited by the user's physical condition,[25] that is to say, that the user needs to be physically capable of performing the technique they are trying to replicate. They need to meet any physical or chakra amount prerequisites the technique in question may have. This ability is also incapable of replicating Kekkei Genkai, due to either not possessing the genetic predisposition and/or having the necessary chakra natures. Naturally, the user also needs to actually see the technique they are attempting to copy, meaning they cannot copy something they did not see or is obscured from their line of sight. In fact, the same can be said for essentially all of the Sharingan's abilities; they are sight based abilities, so if the opponent can obscure the user's vision using extremely thick mist/dust[14] or blind them, the Sharingan's abilities will be rendered useless. Likewise if the opponent is too fast for the Sharingan to visually keep track of them, they will be safe from its abilities. Sharingan Genjutsu requires eye contact, so if the opponent is able to avoid eye contact or is blind, they will be able to avoid Sharingan Genjutsu entirely. Finally, the Sharingan's predictive capabilities can be somewhat circumvented by attacks that have little to no pattern, such as Naruto's chakra arms[102] that emerge from any part of his body and attack instinctively. Use of the Curse Mark erodes the user’s body until they eventually lose themselves, binding them to Orochimaru.[103] The second level makes this erosion speed up dramatically[104] and it requires a greater amount of chakra to sustain it.[105] It also takes a serious toll on the user, leaving them unable to move their body afterwards.[106]

Notable Attacks/Techniques:

Hide/Show

Sharingan (Mirror Wheel Eye): The first ability that Sharingan possesses is the ability to visualize chakra flow with colors, enabling the wielder to differentiate them clearly. The second most notable ability of Sharingan is that it allows the user to perceive with extraordinary clarity, picking up on minute details. Users will be able to track moving objects and anticipate an attacker's next move as Sharingan evolves. The most well-known ability of Sharingans is their ability to copy or counter nearly any technique they meet. Sharingan's final ability is a form of mind control via eye contact.

  • Sharingan Genjutsu: The Uchiha clan's general illusion technique. The Uchiha can lock their opponents into a genjutsu of their choice by simply looking into their eyes.

Katon (Fire Release) One of the basic elemental nature transformations. It is performed by molding superheated chakra inside the stomach before releasing it via the lungs and mouth. There are also variants to this in some mediums, such as gun powder, explosive tags, and chakra flow into a weapon.

  • Great Fireball: An expulsion of fire from the mouth, either as a massive orb of roaring flames or as a continuous flame-thrower attack. Controlling the chakra volume alters the attack's scope.
  • Phoenix Sage Fire: This technique generates a volley of small fireballs that are launched unpredictably at the enemy. The flame's size can also be adjusted individually.
  • Dragon Fire: The user breathes fire along a cord or other long object, which rushes forward in a straight line, burning the enemy down.

Raiton (Lightning Release): Techniques that enable the user to generate lightning and high-frequency vibrations, allowing faster movements and more destructive attacks. The electricity paralyzes the target, making them vulnerable to a finishing strike. Lightning can be infused into weapons or the user's body parts for increased destruction power through vibrations, with the added effect of numbing.

  • Chidori: This technique channels a large amount of lightning chakra to the user's hand. Once the technique is completed, the user charges forward and thrusts the Chidori into the target.

Shunshin no Jutsu (Body Flicker Technique): A high-speed movement technique, accomplished by using chakra to temporarily vitalize the body and move in a short burst of enhanced speed.

Note: "CS1" stands for Curse Seal Level 1 and "CS2" stands for Curse Seal Level 2.
