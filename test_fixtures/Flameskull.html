Summary[]

Flameskulls are undead guardians crafted from the remains of deceased wizards. When created, they begin cackling in a disturbing manner, and fly about the lair of their new master, patrolling for intruders. It maintains very little recollection of its former life, and is instead totally devoted to its new master. They are exceptionally hard to destroy, as they simply reform unless doused in holy water, making them an exceptional threat to the underprepared adventurer.

Name: Flameskull

Attack Potency: Small Building level+ (Listed as a 5th level spellcaster, making them comparable to users of Conjure Barrage)

Speed: Hypersonic+ with High Hypersonic+ reactions (Capable of dodging lightning)

Lifting Strength: Below Average Human (Capable of pushing approximately 22 kg)

Striking Strength: Small Building level+

Durability: Small Building level+

Stamina: Infinite; undead can continue to act without any form of sustenance or rest

Range: Up to Hundreds of Meters with spells such as fireball

Standard Equipment: None notable

Intelligence: Varies, generally between Gifted and Genius; Flameskulls are born from highly skilled spellcasters, and tend to maintain an impressive collection of knowledge despite the mental scarring

Weaknesses: Effects that dispel magic could outright kill them, holy water can negate their regenerative properties
