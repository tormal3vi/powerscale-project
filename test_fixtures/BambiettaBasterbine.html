Summary[]

Bambietta Basterbine (バンビエッタ・バスターバイン, Banbietta Basutābain?) is a Quincy and a member of the Wandenreich's Sternritter with the designation "E" - "The Explode".

The Explode (爆撃ジ・エクスプロード, Ji Ekusupurōdo?): E - The Explode is Bambietta's Schrift which gives her the ability to turn anything at all that she fires her Reishi into, into a bomb. She has what is essentially an unlimited supply of bombs, therefore, turns whatever she strikes with her bullets of Reishi into her own personal armory.

Hirenkyaku (飛ひ廉れん脚きゃく?): Essentially the Quincy version of the Shinigami Shunpo and Arrancar Sonído. The Quincy creates platforms of Reishi under their feet to swiftly move around the battlefield.

  • Blut (血装ブルート, Burūto?): An advanced Quincy technique which grants one inhuman defensive and offensive capabilities, however it can't boost both offensive and defensive simultaneously.
    • Blut Vene (静血装ブルート・ヴェーネ, Burūto Vēne?): The defensive form of Blut, which grants the user a boost in defense by strengthening their skin in a similar fashion to an Arrancar's Hierro. Additionally, Blut Vene can be used to stop blood loss due to injury.
    • Blut Arterie (動血装ブルート・アルテリエ, Burūto Aruterie?): The offensive form of Blut which grants the user inhuman attack power. This technique is the only thing capable of ensuring a Quincy's attacks can significantly damage a Bankai-wielding Shinigami.

Sklaverei (聖隷スクラヴェライ, Sukuraverai?): Vollständig's basic Reishi Absorption ability pushed to the absolute limit. This allows the user to absorb the Reishi that composes spiritual and living beings, an absolute subordination of Reishi. Destroying the Stern Ritter's Halo is the only way to stop it.

Ranso Tengai (乱らん装そう天てん傀がい, Ransōtengai?): An advanced Quincy technique created for injured/old Quincy in order to control their limbs with strings of Reishi like a puppet. Broken bones and severed tendons mean nothing to this as the strings will control the limbs until the body/limb is entirely destroyed.

Shadow (影かげ, Kage?): A teleportation ability used by members of the Wandenreich, this technique can be used by manifesting the user's shadows in front of them, creating a portal that acts as a gate between the Schatten Bereich and other dimensions. The size of these portals can be extended to allow many others to pass through.

Medallion (メダリオン, Medarion?): A device possessed by select members of the Wandenreich military, including all Sternritter which allows one to steal a Shinigami's Bankai or any power that is fundamentally similar to a Shinigami's Bankai.

Reishi Sword: Bambietta can manipulate the Reishi in the air to create swords to use in combat.

  • Quincy Vollständig: Zofiel (滅却師完聖体クインシー・フォルシュテンディッヒー『神の炎ゾフィエル』, Kuinshī Forushutendihhi・Zofieru?): The Wandenreich's improvement on the Letzt Stile, with all the advancements and none of the drawbacks. It allows the user to absorb Reishi from their environment at a rate beyond their limits. This can be used for more powerful attacks, wings for faster flight, enhancing the effects of Schrift (including access to Schrift abilities) and a near endless supply of fuel for abilities like Blut, Hirenkyaku and Heilig Pfeil. In this form, she is be able to naturally fly and her Explode ability is increased dramatically in power.
  • Bankai: Kokujō Tengen Myō'ō (卍ばん解かい「黒こく縄じょう天てん元げん明みょう王おう」?): Kokujo Tengen Myo'o: Komamura's Bankai enables her to completely summon a Giant suit of armor.

Note: Quincy cannot use their stolen Bankai in conjunction with their Vollständig and vice versa
