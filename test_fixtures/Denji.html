Summary[]

Denji (デンジ?) is the main protagonist of the first part of the manga and anime series Chainsaw Man. He is a teenage devil hunter with the exceptional ability to transform into a devil himself.

After the death of his parents, Denji found himself the sole inheritor of the debts they owed to the Yakuza. Left on his own after being given the choice of either paying off the debt or being killed by the Yakuza, Denji would soon run into an injured devil. This was the Chainsaw Devil, Pochita. Realizing each other's predicament, Denji formed a contract with Pochita: in exchange for some of his blood, the devil would aid in his quest to slay devils to make money. Denji and Pochita eventually formed a close friendship while Denji used Pochita to work as a devil hunter for the local Yakuza.

However, this would only last for so long, as the Yakuza eventually betrayed Denji, dismembering him and Pochita and throwing their remains in a dumpster as a sacrifice to the Zombie Devil. Pochita then formed a new contract with Denji: in exchange for Pochita becoming his heart, Denji was to continue chasing the dreams he told to Pochita during their time together. Fusing with Pochita and gaining his devil powers, Denji is reborn as the titular Chainsaw Man, a Devil-human hybrid, and easily slays his betrayers. In order to honor Pochita's last wish, Denji becomes a Public Safety Devil Hunter after meeting Makima, a high-ranking Public Safety member who offers Denji the position due to her interest in his abilities.

Intelligence:

  • General Intelligence
  • Combat Intelligence

At least Average. Due to his impoverished upbringing and incessant life as a devil hunter, Denji has been severely debilitated with his formal education - having never gone to a school before; showing trouble understanding basic kanji[78][79] and the meanings of principal terms during the beginning of Part 1,[13] although this inadvertently has lead to him being able to stay calm in most high-pressure situations.[80] However, it's best to describe Denji as simply untaught rather than actually dumb. He has been shown to already be able to quickly perform long addition in his head at the very start of the series[12] and since then has cognitively progressed by a good amount thanks to being placed under Aki's care,[81] training with Kishibe,[23] watching educational television every single day[18][82] and going to a proper high-school by the end of Part 1.[83] Notably, Denji has shown to possess an incredible attention to detail, e.g. being able to quickly catch onto Power's contradiction of speech when she attempted to lure him into a trap,[33] and recall information even in situations where it seems that he isn't paying any attention at all.[84] Through his perceptiveness, he's able to turn the information he's noticed into strategic or tactical plans when needed. Moreover, Denji has also shown to be able to master various activities in a relatively short amount of time, this can be seen with him learning how to swim after just one short lesson with Reze[27] and being able to produce a wide-range of appetizing culinary dishes after just a few months.[83] His experience living out in the streets has also greatly helped him practically in situations such as making money, e.g. recycling cigarette butts in order to make money from the homeless[85] and exploiting the Eternity Devil's loop to collect duplicating student hats in order to sell them later.[86]

Gifted. Even before his proper training, Denji has shown to be an extremely practical fighter thanks to being in the devil-hunting business ever since he was still a child.[12] He's perfectly willing to aim for the weak points of an opponent's body right at the start of a fight and exploit those injured areas.[11] Denji's ability in combat has since greatly improved thanks to his special training with the likes of Kishibe,[22] a veteran devil hunter who's skilled enough to briefly keep up with Quanxi,[87] another hunter that's skilled enough to be regarded to be able to win against anyone else in the world in a pure bare-knuckle fighting.[88] Through his training, Denji has learnt and later practically applied lessons such as: to always keep a cool head while in battle, be perspective if the environment around him can be exploited against an opponent[23] and to never trust what a "hunter" says.[89] Through Denji may appear naive and dense sometimes, his quick-thinking and outright luscious ingenuity in battle has let him overcome insurmountable situations (examples shown below in 'Feats - Intelligence'). Moreover, he's also able to formulate complex plans that utilises the knowledge he's gained about his opponent with resources he has access to when push comes to shove, as seen in the final battle of the Public Safety Arc, where he managed to take Makima by surprise and successfully outsmart her.[30][83]

Standard Tactics:

  • Human Form
  • Hybrid Form

While Denji typically doesn't prefer to transform into his Hybrid form due to having to deal with the blood loss after he transforms back,[33] he has no qualms transforming when the situation really calls for it. Due to his early experience devil-hunting, Denji has shown to be perfectly willing to end fights as quickly as possible such as targeting areas such the neck for a swift kill.[13] While early on into the series, when faced against male opponents, he would typically try to aim for their nuts first,[11] he has since refined much of his close-quarters combat skills[24] through his training with Kishibe.

When transformed, Denji way of combat changes more towards rushing down his opponents, viscously slashing away at them while relying on his own ability to dodge, endure or his opponent's own blood to regenerate from any attack that's thrown his way.[14][18][90] While Denji does get "battle high" from combat,[91] he still has shown to be perfectly able to adapt to his opponent and strategise against them mid-fight.

Weaknesses: Possesses all general weaknesses possessed by Hybrids. As abilities such as his innate regeneration and transformation are linked to the amount of blood he has in his body,[4][92] if he's running low on blood, he will be unable to regenerate[8] or maintain his transformation.[14] This isn't helped by the fact that just his transformation will cause him lose a lot of blood due to cutting himself with his chainsaws[2] to the point where he'll get incredibly faint[33] and can even pass out after a long battle when reverted back to human form.[79] Likewise, his trigger-based regeneration requires him to pull on the cord on his chest in order for it to work. Can be manipulated to do favours by money or sexual favours from women he finds pretty[93][94] even if they're actively trying to kill him[45] or he himself knows that it's a honey trap.[24] Furthermore, will actively attempt to save pretty women or animals when they're in danger.[93][95] He's also prone to swallow anything with nutritional value that enters his mouth.[96]

Notable Attacks/Techniques:

  • Hybrid Transformation: Through pulling the chainsaw cord that's on his chest, Denji can transform into his Hybrid-Devil form;[1] gaining a plethora of new abilities and also healing/resurrecting him from any wound previously inflicted on him.[2][97]
Transformation shown in action
  • Starter Cord Pulling: Beyond just for transformation, Denji can also pull his starter cord to reprocess the blood already in himself to get back into peak condition[8] even after death,[77] as well as rev his chainsaws even faster than before.
Revival via starter cord pulling shown
  • Chainsaw Creation: True to his namesake, Denji is able to manifest chainsaws out of his head, arms,[77] and later even his legs & feet which he can use to stick onto walls.[16][25] Aside from the one of his head, these chainsaws can also be retracted at will.[8]
Denji manifesting chainsaws out of his arms
Denji manifesting a chainsaw out of his leg
  • Chain Manipulation: By disconnecting the chains off his chainsaws[26] or turning parts of his body tissue into chainsaw chains,[27] Denji can use them like horse reins[17] or wrap around his opponent to pull them closer to himself, which he can follow up with a slicing kick.[18]

Note: While Denji's "Red Chainsaw Man" and Pochita's "Black Chainsaw Man" are technically the same being, it has been decided to separate them into separate profiles. This is because Denji has no control over his "Black Chainsaw Man" form as it's Pochita that takes over.[30] He himself doesn't consider this form to be him[57] and likewise, characters like Makima and Fami also consider Denji's "Red Chainsaw Man" and Pochita's "Black Chainsaw Man" to be completely different beings.[98][99] As a result, this profile only focuses on the titular "Red Chainsaw Man" we see throughout most of the series while Pochita's "Black Chainsaw Man" can be found on Pochita's own profile.
