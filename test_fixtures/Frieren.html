Summary[]

Frieren is the primary protagonist of Frieren: Beyond Journey's End. Eighty years after defeating the demon king as a member of the hero Himmel's party, she travels the world to discover new magic while visiting places where her party traveled during their 10 year voyage to defeat the demon king, in an attempt to relive those past memories, learn more about her past comrades, and discover why such a small time period of her life could have left such a profound impact on her.

They also momentarily gain powers in much the same way, and if they believe they can penetrate or break through something, they often times will.
