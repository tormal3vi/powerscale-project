Powers and Stats[]

Yomi is the father of Shura and one of the Three Kings of the Demon Realm alongside Raizen and Mukuro.

He is the final opponent of Yusuke in the Three Kings Saga.

Powers and Stats[]

Tier: At least 5-C

Name: Yomi

Origin: Yu Yu Hakusho

Gender: Male

Age: Over 1000 years

Classification: Demon

Powers and Abilities: Superhuman Physical Characteristics, Longevity, Acrobatics, Flight, Enhanced Senses and Extrasensory Perception (Can hear everything going on in his country's borders to make up for his blindness), Skilled Martial Artist, Aura, Ki Manipulation, Energy Manipulation (Also fire and electricity based), Air Manipulation (By releasing a dark-green tornado), Absorption (Has the ability to absorb evil energy), Lamarckian Evolution (Grew two new sets of ears after he became blind), Forcefield Creation

Attack Potency: At least Moon level (One of the most powerful Upper S-class beings in Demon World. His offensive power is at 382,500, in comparison to Hokushin who's at 5,900, and should be about 64.8 times stronger than him. Was stated to be capable of wiping out a team of weaker mid to upper S-class demons consisting of Yusuke Urameshi, Kurama, Chū, Rinku, Jin, Toya, Suzuki, and Shishiwakamaru; only didn't go through with it because it would have expended around half of his total ki, which would have left him vulnerable to an approaching Mukuro & Hiei)

Speed: Massively Hypersonic (Comparable to Yusuke)

Lifting Strength: At least Class T

Striking Strength: At least Moon level

Durability: At least Moon level (Roughly scaling from his offensive power. His durability should be on a level several dozen times that of Sensui. Capable of effortlessly defending against attacks from the likes of Shura, who himself has power that should utterly dwarf that of Sensui), higher with barriers/forcefields against Demon Energy based attacks

Stamina: Superhuman (Can fight with Yusuke for about 60 hours in the Manga and managed to win in the end)

Range: Standard Melee Range normally, Tens of Kilometers with energy blasts.

Weaknesses: None notable

Standard Equipment: None notable

Intelligence: Gifted. Is a highly skilled and experienced combatant and veteran statesman. Has over a thousand years of experience as a warrior, military leader, and ruler of Demon World's most technologically advanced nation.

Notable Attacks / Techniques:

  • Ancient Devil Spell Teardown Shock Absorber: Also known as Demon Energy Absorption Wall, Yomi creates a spherical barrier that allows him to absorb demon energy.
  • Tornado Energy Blast: Yomi's fists are surrounded by an energy orb and he fires a large dark-green tornado inflicting great damage to his enemy.
  • Energy Blast: Yomi fires multiple successive shots from the palm of his hand.
